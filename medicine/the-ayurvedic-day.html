The Ayurvedic Day: Notes on Dinacharya

9 November 2026
❦

Ayurveda places great weight on the shape of an ordinary day. Long before anyone spoke of "habits" or "routines", the classical texts described dinacharya — the daily rhythm of waking, cleansing, eating, working and resting in step with the natural world.
