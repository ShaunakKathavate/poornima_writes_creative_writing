The Monsoon Letter

A story · 7 November 2026

The envelope was soft with age, its stamp a colour the post office no longer printed. The postman held it out apologetically, as if he himself had kept it waiting all these years.

She did not open it at once. She set it on the windowsill beside the tulsi pot and watched the rain come down over the lane, the way it had that summer, the summer the letter was written.
